# Late to the chronology, slots 1976-1984: sourced inventory

Companion to [issue #1](https://github.com/arivero/qcdstring/issues/1)
(label `late-to-chronology`), following the closure plan posted there on
26 November 2026. The issue keeps the original list, written from memory;
this file gives each item a dated disposition from primary sources. The
convention is in `OUTPUTS_PLAN.md`, "Lost and late".

**How the sources were read (26 November 2026).** *Text*: the arXiv full
text was opened and the quoted number or statement found in it. *Abstract*:
only the INSPIRE record and its abstract were read. *Metadata*: only the
bibliographic record (authors, title, journal, dates) was read.
"Preprint" is the arXiv submission month or, before arXiv, the INSPIRE
preprint date; "journal" is the volume year. A *proposed* measurement is one
our papers name and leave undone; a *performed* one is published by others.

**Priority.** For each row the dated comparison gives only the interval
between our slot and the earliest verified matching result. An earlier
matching result may exist; "earliest verified" means earliest among the
sources checked here. No row claims priority for this line.

## L1. Excited levels of the closed tube, and the phase shift read from them

* **Observable.** Energies of the excited states of a closed flux tube wound
  around a periodic direction of length L, and the two-phonon scattering phase
  extracted from their L dependence.
* **Our slot and passage.** 1984, in the three-dimensional Z2 theory, with the
  antiperiodic dual-Ising lattices of QCDS-82/1, Section 3.2. Proposed, never
  performed: QCDS-84/1 computes the phase that such levels would show,
  2 delta = 2.4 rad for the lowest two-phonon state at L sqrt(sigma) = 2.6
  (`outputs/1984-critical-dimension/paper.md:493-497`). The measurement was
  dropped when the line turned to theory (`OUTPUTS_PLAN.md:42`).
* **Performed by others.**
  * A. Athenodorou, B. Bringoltz, M. Teper, "The closed string spectrum of
    SU(N) gauge theories in 2+1 dimensions",
    [arXiv:0709.0693](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.0693), preprint September
    2007, Phys. Lett. B 656 (2007) 132. About 30 lowest states for lengths
    0.45-3 fm. *Abstract.*
  * The same authors, "Closed flux tubes and their string description in
    D=3+1 SU(N) gauge theories",
    [arXiv:1007.4720](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.4720), preprint July 2010,
    JHEP 02 (2011) 030. About 20 states up to l sqrt(sigma) ~ 6. *Abstract.*
  * S. Dubovsky, R. Flauger, V. Gorbenko,
    [arXiv:1301.2325](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.2325), preprint January
    2013, Phys. Rev. Lett. 111 (2013) 062006. Figure 3 gives the two-phonon
    phase shifts in the symmetric traceless, scalar and pseudoscalar channels,
    inverted from the SU(3), D = 3+1 spectra of arXiv:1007.4720; the
    pseudoscalar phase passes through pi/2. *Text.*
  * The same authors, [arXiv:1404.0037](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.0037),
    preprint March 2014, J. Exp. Theor. Phys. 120 (2015) 399. The method
    applied to the D = 2+1 spectra as well. *Abstract.*
  * C. Chen, P. Conkey, S. Dubovsky, G. Hernández-Chifflet,
    [arXiv:1808.01339](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01339), preprint August
    2018, Phys. Rev. D 98 (2018) 114024. Phase-shift extraction with
    polarization effects included through the TTbar dressing. *Abstract.*
* **Match.** Different theory. With one transverse direction (d = 3) there
  is a single two-phonon channel, so the channel-resolved phase of 2013 has
  no Z2 analogue; the nearest d = 3 analysis is that of 2014. An INSPIRE title
  search found no measurement of excited closed-tube levels in the
  three-dimensional Z2 theory. The exact-theory date is unresolved.
* **Disposition.** Late. Earliest verified matching result: 2007 (levels,
  SU(N), D = 2+1), 23 years after the slot.

## L2. Width of the tube growing as the logarithm of its length

* **Observable.** Mean squared transverse width at the midpoint of the tube
  between static sources (the midplane of a planar Wilson loop), against the
  separation R, in the rough phase: w^2 = (1/2 pi sigma) ln(R/R_c).
* **Our slot and passage.** QCDS-80/1, Section 5, measured the closed-interface
  version (Ising interfaces on L x L x 24 boxes, Table 4) and stated the
  open-tube version as a prediction it did not measure
  (`outputs/1980-excited-flux-tube/paper.md:297-300`). The issue places the
  measurement at 1982 with the antiperiodic lattices of QCDS-82/1. Those
  lattices give the closed-interface geometry again; the open tube requires
  Wilson-loop (frustrated-link) runs, which is the geometry of the
  measurements below.
* **Prediction already in print.** M. Lüscher, G. Münster, P. Weisz, "How
  thick are chromoelectric flux tubes?", preprint July 1980, Nucl. Phys. B 180
  (1981) 1. *Metadata.*
* **Performed by others.**
  * M. Caselle, F. Gliozzi, U. Magnea, S. Vinti, "The width of the color flux
    tube", [arXiv:hep-lat/9411060](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9411060),
    INSPIRE preprint date September 1994 (arXiv 28 November 1994), Nucl.
    Phys. B Proc. Suppl. 42 (1995) 222. Square Wilson loops in the 3d Z2
    gauge theory: sides 16-40 at beta = 0.7516 and 15-60 at beta = 0.7460;
    fitted slopes 14.4(1.2) and 7.7(6) against the predicted 14.8 and 8.42.
    The text states that the logarithmic growth "has not yet been observed
    until now". *Text.*
  * The same authors, "Width of long color flux tubes in lattice gauge
    systems", [arXiv:hep-lat/9510019](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9510019),
    preprint October 1995, Nucl. Phys. B 460 (1996) 397. Three couplings,
    square and rectangular loops up to 60 x 60 (Table II); universal fit
    a = 0.150(5) against 1/2 pi = 0.159, from sqrt(sigma) R ~ 1.2. *Text.*
  * Closed-interface geometry: M. Hasenbusch, K. Pinn,
    [arXiv:hep-lat/9211010](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9211010), preprint
    November 1992, Nucl. Phys. B Proc. Suppl. 30 (1993) 857; the 1995 paper
    uses their Physica A 192 (1993) 342 data. *Metadata.* This is the geometry
    QCDS-80/1 had already measured in its own slot.
* **Match.** Exact for the 1994 report and the 1995 paper.
* **Disposition.** Late. Earliest verified matching measurement: preprint
  1994, 12 years after the 1982 slot. The prediction is from 1980 and belongs
  to the cited paper; this line passed over only the measurement.

## L3. Hagedorn fit of the hadron tables with the string's level density

* **Observable.** A fit of the observed level density with the string's own
  form rho(m) ~ m^(-(d-1)/2) exp(m/T_H), power -3/2 in d = 4, left for later
  in QCDS-82/1 (`outputs/1982-hagedorn-deconfinement/paper.md:173-177`); and
  the meson/baryon comparison of ledger entry P16 (`PREDICTIONS.md:27`).
* **Performed by others.**
  * K. R. Dienes, J.-R. Cudell, "Conformal invariance and degrees of freedom in
    the QCD string", [arXiv:hep-th/9309126](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9309126),
    INSPIRE preprint date August 1993 (arXiv September 1993), Phys. Rev. Lett.
    72 (1994) 187. Degeneracy g ~ (M/T_H)^(-2B) exp(M/T_H) with
    B = (3 + D_perp)/4, hence rho = 2 alpha' M g ~ M^(-3/2) exp(M/T_H) for
    D_perp = 2, with Bessel-function subleading terms; meson states 0.3-1.7
    GeV; best fit T_H = 300 MeV, c = 1.97 at alpha' = 0.85 GeV^-2. *Text.*
  * Separate meson and baryon fits: W. Broniowski, W. Florkowski,
    [arXiv:hep-ph/0004104](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0004104), preprint
    April 2000, Phys. Lett. B 490 (2000) 223. With the Hagedorn power
    (m^2 + m_0^2)^(-5/4) and m_0 = 0.5 GeV: T_mes = 197 MeV,
    T_bar = 141 MeV. *Text.* Update: W. Broniowski, W. Florkowski,
    L. Ya. Glozman, [arXiv:hep-ph/0407290](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0407290),
    Phys. Rev. D 70 (2004) 117503. *Abstract.*
* **Match.** Exact for mesons (1993). For P16 no fit in the string form was
  found; the 2000 fits use the Hagedorn power and give unequal temperatures.
  P16 stays unresolved.
* **Disposition.** Late. Earliest verified: preprint 1993, 11 years after the
  slot. The issue's remembered "1994-2000" becomes 1993 (preprint), 1994
  (journal).

## L4. Which side of T_H the deconfinement temperature falls on

* **Observable.** Continuum T_c / sqrt(sigma) against the string's
  T_H = sqrt(3 sigma / pi (d-2)) = 0.6910 sqrt(sigma) in d = 4, for SU(2) and
  SU(3). QCDS-82/1 found 0.92-0.96 of T_H at N_t = 3 and 4 and left the
  continuum side open (`outputs/1982-hagedorn-deconfinement/paper.md:784-787`);
  ledger entry P07 (`PREDICTIONS.md:19`).
* **Performed by others.** The ratios to T_H are our arithmetic on the quoted
  values.
  * J. Fingberg, U. M. Heller, F. Karsch,
    [arXiv:hep-lat/9208012](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9208012), preprint
    August 1992, Nucl. Phys. B 392 (1993) 493. SU(2): 0.69(2), so
    T_c/T_H = 1.00(3), which does not decide. *Abstract.*
  * G. Boyd et al., [arXiv:hep-lat/9602007](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9602007),
    preprint January 1996, Nucl. Phys. B 469 (1996) 419. SU(3) continuum:
    0.629(3), so T_c/T_H = 0.910(4), below. *Abstract.*
  * B. Lucini, M. Teper, U. Wenger,
    [arXiv:hep-lat/0307017](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0307017), preprint
    July 2003, JHEP 01 (2004) 061, Table 6. SU(2): 0.7091(36), so
    T_c/T_H = 1.026(5), above. SU(3): 0.6462(30), so 0.935(4), below.
    SU(infinity): 0.5960(41), so 0.863(6). *Text.*
  * The same authors, [arXiv:hep-lat/0502003](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0502003),
    preprint February 2005, JHEP 02 (2005) 033: second order for SU(2),
    weakly first order for SU(3). *Abstract.*
* **Match.** Exact.
* **Disposition.** Late. SU(3) is decided by the 1996 preprint (below T_H).
  SU(2) is decided by the 2003 preprint, and on the side above T_H, a third
  possibility beside the "below or at" that QCDS-82/1 left open. Earlier SU(3)
  continuum estimates were not checked.

* **Observable.** Pomeron intercept and slope from total cross-sections

    [arXiv:hep-ph/9209205](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9209205), preprint

## L9. The first gap of the open tube at R sqrt(sigma) >= 2.5 to 1%

* **Observable.** E_1 - E_0 of the tube between static sources at
  R sqrt(sigma) >= 2.5, to 1%. At R sqrt(sigma) = 2.5 the square-root gap is
  1.056 sqrt(sigma); an R^-4 end term raises it by 1.1-6.1% and an R^-2 term
  lowers it by 3.5% (`outputs/1984-critical-dimension/paper.md:416-421`). Our
  theory: 3d Z2 at beta = 0.73, which means R = 12.
* **Performed by others.**
  * M. Caselle, M. Hasenbusch, M. Panero,
    [arXiv:hep-lat/0510107](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0510107), preprint
    October 2005, JHEP 01 (2006) 076. 3d Z2 by duality; open-string levels from
    Polyakov-loop correlators at r = 4, 9, 19 and 29 (r sqrt(sigma) ~ 0.8,
    1.9, 4.0 and 6.1 at sigma = 0.044023(3)), Figure 11 out to
    r sqrt(sigma) ~ 10. The gap lies above the square root at short distance
    and converges to it, with the excess still visible at r = 19. The gaps are
    given in figures only. *Text.*
  * B. B. Brandt, [arXiv:1010.3625](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.3625), preprint
    October 2010, JHEP 02 (2011) 040. 3d SU(2), beta = 5.0,
    sigma = 0.0975(2). Table 3: Delta E_10 = 0.340(2) at R = 8
    (R sqrt(sigma) = 2.50), a 0.6% error, and to R = 12
    (R sqrt(sigma) = 3.75) at 1.2%. In units of sqrt(sigma) the gap at 2.50 is
    1.089(6), 3.1(6)% above the square root. Fit b_2 = -0.5(2)(2) assuming
    R^-4; R^-6 not excluded. *Text.*
  * B. B. Brandt, [arXiv:1705.03828](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03828),
    preprint May 2017, JHEP 07 (2017) 008. 3d SU(2) and SU(3) potentials:
    leading correction exponent 4; continuum boundary coefficient. *Abstract.*
  * Theory for context: M. Lüscher, P. Weisz,
    [arXiv:hep-th/0406205](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0406205) (June 2004):
    corrections of order 1/r^2 vanish. O. Aharony, M. Field,
    [arXiv:1008.2636](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2636) (August 2010): the
    leading open-string deviation is 1/R^4. M. Billó et al.,
    [arXiv:1202.1984](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.1984) (February 2012): the
    boundary action tested on 3d Ising-gauge Wilson loops. *Abstract.*
* **Match.** The 2010 SU(2) data meet the distance and the precision. The
  coefficient b_2 is non-universal, so the Z2 value at beta = 0.73 needs a Z2
  gap; the 2005 Z2 data fix the sign of the excess (the R^-4 direction)
  without a tabulated 1% value at R sqrt(sigma) = 2.5.
* **Disposition.** Late. Earliest verified: 2005 (Z2, sign), 2010 (SU(2),
  distance and precision).

## A1. Related result in a different theory: the 1991 Abelian Higgs tube

QCDS-91/S1, Section 10.2 (`outputs/1991-vortex-pseudoscalar/paper.md:441-453`),
computes on a periodic Abelian Higgs tube the first isolated crossing of the
odd pole with two phonons, m_* L_1 = 4 pi, with minimum separation
2|v_A|/m_* = 0.08385 e at tree level; the odd state has zero trace coupling,
and at critical coupling the degenerate even partner crosses in the trace
channel with a gap of the same leading size. It is a result of this line in
its own slot, so it carries no late status. Its Yang-Mills counterpart is the
channel-resolved phase of 2013 in L1. No search for an Abelian Higgs
counterpart in the literature was made for this inventory.

## F1-F2. The two 1986 carry-forwards

* **F1.** The direct area-action level at order L^-5 in three dimensions
  (QCDS-84/1, `outputs/1984-critical-dimension/paper.md:505-511`).
* **F2.** The zero-point and open-string rules of QCDS-84/1, Section 4.2,
  beyond first order (`outputs/1984-critical-dimension/paper.md:463-487`).
* **What the 1986 paper did.** It records both as unfinished
  (`outputs/1986-fermionic-flux-tube/paper.md:201-203` and `1303-1306`), and
  Appendix C states that expanding the square root does not derive the L^-5
  term (`paper.md:1414-1420`). The 1988 paper carries them again
  (`outputs/1988-thickness-curvature/paper.md:646-648`) and the 1990 row of
  `OUTPUTS_PLAN.md` lists them as carried over. The 1990 working note, the
  1991, 1992 and 1995 papers and the 1994 working calculations do not take
  them up.
* **Published by others.**
  * F1: O. Aharony, E. Karzbrun, [arXiv:0903.1927](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.1927),
    preprint March 2009, JHEP 06 (2009) 012: in D = 3 the closed-string levels
    agree with Nambu-Goto at least to 1/L^5. O. Aharony, Z. Komargodski,
    [arXiv:1302.6257](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.6257), preprint February
    2013, JHEP 05 (2013) 118: terms through 1/L^5 universal, with a universal
    deviation at 1/L^5 for excited strings in D > 3. An unpublished preprint,
    J. M. Drummond, [arXiv:hep-th/0411017](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0411017)
    (November 2004), argues universality of the subleading terms. *Abstract.*
  * F2: S. Dubovsky, R. Flauger, V. Gorbenko,
    [arXiv:1205.6805](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6805), preprint May 2012,
    JHEP 09 (2012) 133: the exact S-matrix and the thermodynamic Bethe ansatz
    reconstruct the complete finite-volume closed-string spectrum. M. Caselle,
    D. Fioravanti, F. Gliozzi, R. Tateo,
    [arXiv:1305.1278](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.1278), preprint May 2013,
    JHEP 07 (2013) 071: the same on a strip, giving the open Nambu-Goto
    spectrum. *Abstract.*
* **Disposition.** Carried and open inside this line. The march has reached
  1995, before 2009, so neither item is late or lost yet. F1 becomes late if
  the line passes 2009 without it, F2 if it passes 2012-13.

## C1-C4. Controls: published by the world before our slot
