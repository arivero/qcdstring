# 7 November 1974

The long march. Yesterday's thesis covers what can be defended with
1974 data and 1975 computation. Today's document, `LONG_MARCH.md`, is
about the part of the memory that cannot: five consistent superstring
theories, a web of dualities among them, an eleven-dimensional theory
behind the web, and compactification geometries of a sophistication no
hadron physicist will ever need. I have written down the admission in
its first section. At that point the subject is geometry.

What I claim is that the geometry is the geometry of the confining
vacuum. Every place where the duality web touches a confining gauge
theory has a hadronic name: the radial direction is the scale, the tip
of the geometry is Lambda, the wrapped branes are baryons and domain
walls, the S-duality is Mandelstam's and 't Hooft's dual superconductor,
the membrane of the eleventh dimension is the flux tube with a
thickness, the geometric transition of a G2 manifold is confinement
itself. The part of the web that touches nothing confining, the
heterotic string on a Calabi-Yau as a theory of everything, is left
alone. Six new ideas, 017 to 022, and three predictions, P15 to P17.

The shelf already had more of this than I expected: Seiberg-Witten,
Hanany-Witten, Hull-Townsend, Witten's various dimensions, Witten's
branes-and-QCD, Atiyah-Witten and Acharya-Gukov on G2, and a 2024 letter
reading the SO(32) scalars as diquarks and mesons. All linked.

One honest line for the ledger. The confining theories with geometric
duals are supersymmetric or nearly so. The bridges to QCD with light
quarks are planar equivalence and the type 0 construction, and both are
approximate. If the march fails, it fails there.
