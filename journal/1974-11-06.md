# 6 November 1974

Founding entry. Repository opened, thesis written, ledger opened with
fourteen predictions. The desk: the April Review of Particle Properties,
Wilson's preprint from Cornell on confinement, 't Hooft's planar paper
and his two-dimensional model, Nambu's strings-monopoles-gauge-fields
paper, Scherk and Schwarz's "Dual models for non-hadrons" with the
spin-2 state circled in red, and the ISR total cross-section plots.

Five days from now a narrow state at 3.1 GeV will be announced at
Brookhaven and at SLAC on the same day. Prediction P01 is registered
before the fact: the state is a bound pair of a new heavy quark, and its
levels are those of a Coulomb-plus-string potential whose string tension
is the one on the Chew-Frautschi plot. `numerics/charmonium.py` is the
calculation; it fixes m_c from the 1S mass and predicts the 2S, the P
centroid, the D and the 3S. The note is to be sent out before any
competing potential model appears.

State of mind. The hardest thing about remembering the future is that
one remembers the answers more clearly than the derivations. The rule
for this repository is that a remembered result is a conjecture. The
only thing the memory is allowed to do is to pick which calculation to
do next. In that role it is worth decades.

The order of business:

1. `regge_fit.py` on the 1974 tables. Universal slope, sigma, T_H.
2. Casimir energy of a string with fixed ends, by hand, with Dirichlet
   modes and a zeta-regulated sum. Then the square-root formula from the
   light-cone gauge. These are 1975 results in the memory's chronology
   (1981 and 1983, in fact) and there is nothing in them that requires
   anything beyond GGRT.
3. A Z2 gauge theory on a 12^3 lattice, by heat bath, on whatever
   machine will have me. The area law is the point; the duality with the
   Ising model is the check. Letter to Wilson about machine time at
   Cornell, and about his renormalisation-group scale as a coordinate.
4. Read Scherk and Schwarz once more, fairly. Their observation that the
   massless spin-2 state couples like a graviton in the zero-slope limit
   is correct. The dispute is about what the state is at alpha' = 0.9
   GeV^-2, and the answer in the memory is: the Pomeron, with its
   intercept moved from 2 to 1.08 by the geometry of the size coordinate.
   The argument needs 002, 006 and 007 before it is anything more than a
   slogan.

Calibration note for the reader in 2026: every number in `PREDICTIONS.md`
can be checked against the literature in `papers/memory-future.tsv`.
That is the point of the ledger.

Evening. First numbers. Slopes: rho 0.89, Delta 0.89, N 1.01, Lambda
0.98, omega 1.00, K* 0.82, phi 0.80 GeV^-2; pooled 0.87, so sigma = 0.184
GeV^2, sqrt(sigma) = 429 MeV, T_H = 296 MeV. The strange trajectories sit
low, which is what a string with heavier endpoints does (008). The
charmonium calculation with that sigma and kappa = 0.52 puts the P-wave
centroid at 3.53 and the 2S at 3.71 GeV once m_c is fixed by the 1S.
Written up as P01 in the ledger, five days early.

Night. The Z2 gauge theory ran on a 12^3 lattice: two hundred sweeps, a
few seconds on the machine of 2026, perhaps an hour on the CDC. The area
law is there. The Creutz ratio follows -ln tanh beta up to beta = 0.4,
leaves it at 0.5, where the Ising duality says the Wilson surface
roughens, and falls toward zero as the dual Ising critical point is
approached. Three numbers, one evening, and the roughening transition
of 1980 is visible as a bend in a table. Tomorrow: the same with U(1)
in four dimensions, and the letter to Wilson.
