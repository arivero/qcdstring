# 017. Type 0 strings are the non-supersymmetric string of QCD
**Status:** open
**Depends on:** 005, 002
**Predictions:** none registered

## Claim
The string dual of a non-supersymmetric confining gauge theory has no
spacetime fermions and a closed-string tachyon. In a background with
Ramond-Ramond flux the tachyon acquires a positive mass and the theory
flows to confinement in the infrared with asymptotic freedom in the
ultraviolet. This is the type 0B construction. Its tachyon is the
vacuum energy of the gauge theory, and its running tachyon profile is
the running coupling.

## Known in 1974
* The bosonic and Neveu-Schwarz tachyons. Nothing else.

## What the memory says
* Polyakov 1998, "The wall of the cave": non-supersymmetric gauge
  theories should be dual to non-supersymmetric strings; type 0 is the
  candidate.
* Klebanov-Tseytlin 1998-99: D3-branes of type 0B; the tachyon is
  stabilised by the flux; the dual gauge theory is SU(N)xSU(N) with
  bifundamental scalars; the coupling runs logarithmically and the
  theory confines.
* The construction never reached pure QCD, and the tachyon of the
  theory in flat space remains. Its honest status in the memory is: a
  proof of concept that the GSO projection is optional.

## Test
1. `[pencil]` Rederive the type 0 spectrum as the diagonal modular
   invariant of the NSR string without the GSO projection; identify the
   doubled Ramond-Ramond sector.
2. `[pencil]` The tachyon effective potential in the presence of flux
   and the condition for a positive mass.
3. `[consistency]` Does the tachyon of 005 (the short-string instability)
   match the type 0 tachyon in the dual? The radial profile should put
   it at the short-distance end.

## Expected outcome, and what would refute it
A consistent non-supersymmetric closed-string sector whose tachyon is
confined to the ultraviolet region. Refuted if flux cannot lift the
tachyon in any confining background.
