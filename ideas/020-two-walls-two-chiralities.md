# 020. Two walls, two chiralities: the heterotic string as the flavoured flux tube
**Status:** open (speculative)
**Depends on:** 008
**Predictions:** none registered

## Claim
In the Sakai-Sugimoto model the left- and right-handed flavour
symmetries U(N_f)_L x U(N_f)_R live on two stacks of eight-branes that
join in the interior of the geometry; the joining is chiral symmetry
breaking. The eight-branes are the objects that orientifold planes of
type I' carry sixteen of, and type I' lifted to eleven dimensions is
Horava-Witten theory: M-theory on an interval with an E8 on each wall.
The hadronic reading: the two walls are the two chiralities of the
quark flavour symmetry, the interval is the direction along which the
flux tube connects a left-handed quark to a right-handed one, and the
heterotic string's internal current algebra is the internal sector
that a flavoured flux tube carries along its length. The SO(32) and
E8xE8 groups are the largest flavour symmetries such a tube can carry.

## Known in 1974
* Chan-Paton factors give U(N) or SO(N) or Sp(N) at the ends (Chan-Paton
  1969, Schwarz 1971); nothing else.

## What the memory says
* Gross-Harvey-Martinec-Rohm 1985: the heterotic string, with E8xE8 or
  SO(32) from a sixteen-dimensional internal lattice on the left-movers.
* Polchinski-Witten 1995: heterotic SO(32) equals type I at strong
  coupling; Horava-Witten 1996: E8xE8 equals M-theory on an interval.
* Sakai-Sugimoto 2004: D8 and anti-D8 in Witten's D4 background; the
  pion as the Wilson line across the joined branes; the Skyrme term.
* Sen 1998: brane-antibrane tachyon condensation, the mechanism by
  which the two stacks join.
* The lattice flux tube in d = 4 carries at least one massive internal
  mode (013). A heterotic-type internal sector is the general version.

## Test
1. `[pencil]` Count the worldsheet degrees of freedom of a flux tube
   with N_f flavours at each end, treating the ends as Chan-Paton
   stacks; find the symmetry of the open string in the orientifolded
   theory.
2. `[pencil]` Reproduce the Sakai-Sugimoto pion from the Wilson line of
   the flavour gauge field across the joined branes; relate f_pi to the
   string tension and the 't Hooft coupling.
3. `[pheno]` The number sixteen: is there a hadronic sense in which
   sixteen is the maximal number of flavours a chirality can carry? The
   memory offers only the conformal window (N_f < 16.5 for SU(3)), which
   is a coincidence until shown otherwise. Record it as such.

## Expected outcome, and what would refute it
A consistent assignment of chiral flavour to two boundaries of a
string's extra direction. Refuted if the pion cannot be obtained from
the junction or if the Horava-Witten lift of the flavour-brane system
fails to exist.
