# 021. Geometry is the vacuum: the geometric transition is confinement
**Status:** open
**Depends on:** 002, 003
**Predictions:** none registered

## Claim
The compactification geometries that describe confining gauge theories
are the vacuum structure of those theories in dual variables. The
cleanest statement: N D6-branes (type IIA) or D5-branes (IIB) wrapped on
a three-sphere of a resolved conifold produce N=1 super Yang-Mills; at
large N the sphere shrinks and a dual three-sphere opens, carrying N
units of flux and no branes. The branes (gluons) have been replaced by
flux (the condensate). The size of the new sphere is the gluino
condensate. In M-theory this is a flop of a G2 holonomy manifold
(Atiyah-Maldacena-Vafa 2001), and N=1 SYM lives at the ADE singularities
of G2 manifolds in general (Acharya 2000, Atiyah-Witten 2001, Acharya-Gukov
2004). The local shelf holds the M-theory side of this literature.

## Known in 1974
* The vacuum of a confining theory is a condensate (the dual
  superconductor); nothing about its geometry.

## What the memory says
* Gopakumar-Vafa 1998: the open-string/closed-string duality on the
  conifold for Chern-Simons theory.
* Vafa 2000: superstrings and topological strings at large N; the
  geometric transition for N=1 SYM; the superpotential from fluxes.
* Atiyah-Maldacena-Vafa 2001: the M-theory flop; the three branches
  related by triality, which are the N vacua and the confining phase.
* Klebanov-Strassler 2000: the deformed conifold with the deformation
  parameter fixed by the condensate; Maldacena-Nunez 2000: wrapped
  fivebranes; both give an area law for the Wilson loop.
* Atiyah-Witten 2001 (local copy, `physres5/references/pdfs/12_...`),
  Acharya-Gukov 2004 (local copy, `11_...`): the general G2 story.

## Test
1. `[pencil]` The Wilson loop in the deformed conifold: a string
   dropped to the tip has tension proportional to the warp factor there,
   which is a power of the deformation parameter; show sigma is a
   function of Lambda alone.
2. `[pencil]` The N vacua as the N ways to place the flux; theta
   dependence; the domain wall as a wrapped brane.
3. `[consistency]` The gluino condensate in these geometries versus the
   weak-coupling instanton computation of the same quantity (Novikov-
   Shifman-Vainshtein-Zakharov 1983). They should match in N dependence.

## Expected outcome, and what would refute it
sigma, Lambda, the condensate and the number of vacua all encoded in one
geometry with no free parameter. Refuted if the geometric and
field-theoretic condensates disagree in their N dependence.
