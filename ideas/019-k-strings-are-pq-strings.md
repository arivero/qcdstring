# 019. k-strings are (p,q) strings and S-duality is the dual superconductor
**Status:** open
**Depends on:** 014, 003
**Predictions:** P10

## Claim
The 1974 picture of confinement as a dual Meissner effect ('t Hooft,
Mandelstam, Nambu) is the S-duality of the string web, seen from the
gauge theory. In softly broken N=2 (Seiberg-Witten 1994) the monopoles
condense and the confining strings are Abrikosov-Nielsen-Olesen
vortices of the dual Abelian theory; the k-string tensions obey the
sine law (Douglas-Shenker 1995). In the IIB duals the fundamental
string, the D-string and the (p,q) bound states are the strings of
charge p electric and q magnetic, and the k-strings of the confining
theory are D-strings or wrapped D3-branes with tension near
sin(pi k/N) (Herzog-Klebanov 2001). Non-Abelian vortices with
orientational moduli (Hanany-Tong 2003, Auzzi et al 2003) are the
precise form of the dual-superconductor string.

## Known in 1974
* 't Hooft and Polyakov monopoles; Nielsen-Olesen vortices; Nambu's
  1974 paper puts the two together: a string is a flux tube in a
  monopole condensate.
* Mandelstam's and 't Hooft's dual-superconductor conjecture is being
  formulated now (1975-76 in the memory).

## What the memory says
* 't Hooft 1978: electric and magnetic order parameters; Z_N.
* Seiberg-Witten 1994 (local copy, `phys4/sources/seiberg-witten-1994.pdf`):
  monopole condensation and confinement, exactly, in N=2 broken to N=1.
* Douglas-Shenker 1995: sine law.
* Lattice: Del Debbio et al 2001, Lucini-Teper-Wenger 2004: sine law
  within a few percent in SU(N) for N up to 8.

## Test
1. `[pencil]` ANO vortex tension in the dual Abelian Higgs model as a
   function of the condensate; the k-vortex and its binding.
2. `[pencil]` The sine law from the SU(N) Seiberg-Witten curve at the
   maximally singular point (Douglas-Shenker).
3. `[lattice]` Monopole currents in the maximal Abelian gauge and their
   correlation with the flux tube (in the memory: the 1990s Kanazawa
   and Pisa programmes). A 1976 version with U(1) in 4d is feasible.

## Expected outcome, and what would refute it
Monopole condensation in the confining phase of U(1) and in the maximal
Abelian projection of SU(2); sine-law k-strings. Refuted by k-string
ratios equal to k in SU(N) or by a confining phase without monopole
condensation.
