3a. Deformation controls on the weak side (tension held fixed)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  tree-level on-shell: sqrt(MZ^2-MW^2) = g' v/2 with v = (sqrt2 G_F)^(-1/2) = 246.2196 GeV, so g' = 0.3500
  R_EW = [alpha(0) g'/2] (v/sqrt(sigma)) = 0.001277 x 582.4
  two-wall family: sqrt(sigma) ~ M e^(-k y1), v ~ M e^(-k y2): d ln R_EW / d(k (y2 - y1)) = -1;
    any R_EW is reached by moving one wall; the string data must fix k (y2 - y1), g' at the wall
    and the running of the photon coupling to the Thomson limit.
  threshold control (second line, FLAVOUR_AND_SCALES.md section 4): at fixed Lambda_6,
    d ln Lambda_3 / d ln v = 2/9; with sqrt(sigma) ~ Lambda_3 and alpha(0), g' fixed,
    d ln R_EW / d ln v = 7/9, d ln R_N / d ln v = 0, d ln(R_N/R_EW) / d ln v = -7/9.
    holding R_N/R_EW to its measured fractional error 0.000347 needs v/Lambda_6 fixed to 0.000447: ordinary QCD supplies no such relation.
