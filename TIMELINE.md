# Timeline from memory, with intervention points

What the memory says happens, year by year, and where a 1974 worker with
that memory could act. Every entry below is `[MEMORY]` except the ones
dated 1974 or earlier. Dates are those of publication; preprints are
typically a year earlier.

## Reading of the timeline

The hadronic string dies in 1977 for lack of three things: a way to live
in d = 4, a way to get hard scattering, and a computer result showing
that gauge theories make strings. All three exist in the memory. Two of
them (the effective string, the Monte Carlo) are 1975 projects. The
third (the warped coordinate) is the hard one and the one on which the
plan may fail.
