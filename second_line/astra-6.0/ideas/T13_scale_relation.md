# T13 — Relate confinement and weak symmetry breaking

**Priority:** P1, from the first cycle. **Status:** transmutation and threshold controls executed; microscopic relation proposed.

Fix the scale dictionary before calculation: weak v from G_F; a specified Λ_QCD convention; a hadronic observable; microscopic M_s and confining √σ_color separately; any KK or compositeness scale. All dimensionful quantities must descend from the candidate's stated inputs.

Try a common microscopic sector whose geometry or dynamics determines both a gauge coupling and the weak vacuum. Integrate the RG equations with the complete spectrum and match across thresholds. Compute or clearly leave uncomputed the conversion from Λ to the chosen hadronic observable.

An alternative is two dynamical scales generated from related couplings at one reference scale M. If Λ_i=M exp[−2π/(b_i α_i(M))], then their ratio follows from the difference of the two exponents. Deriving the relation between α_i matters as much as writing the exponential. Explain how the second condensate yields the weak order parameter and how quarks couple to it.

**Deliverable:** a formula and uncertainty or allowed range for v/Λ or v/√σ_color, with every remaining parameter visible. Compute logarithmic sensitivities and test whether the permitted variations remain predictive.

**Reject the explanatory claim if:** independent wall positions or condensate scales are fitted; M is renamed v without a dynamical relation; the microscopic string tension is conflated with QCD tension; a Planck scale silently supplies the hierarchy; or heavy-threshold dependence is mistaken for a complete determination.

Executed controls: ordinary QCD chiral breaking gives too small a v; one-loop transmutation can produce a moderate logarithmic hierarchy but depends on its coupling; fixed-high-scale threshold matching gives ∂lnΛ₃/∂lnm_t=2/27 and, for three heavy masses proportional to v, ∂lnΛ₃/∂lnv=2/9.

Dependencies: T03–T06 for the strong sector, T10–T12 for actual fields and couplings. Sources: H7/H8, M17–M19. Derivations and qualifications are in notes/FLAVOUR_AND_SCALES.md.
