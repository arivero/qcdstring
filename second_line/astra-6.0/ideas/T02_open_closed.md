# T02 — Test whether the closed sector can be removed

**Priority:** P0. **Type:** imported consistency benchmark; calculation proposed.

Start with ordinary interacting open strings at finite string scale and coupling. Study a nonplanar one-loop amplitude. The annulus has an open-string loop interpretation and a closed-string propagation interpretation after a modular change of variable.

Track the intermediate-state spectrum and residues explicitly. Depending on transverse propagation, the singularities need not all be isolated poles. A restriction to planar diagrams or to tree level is not a complete interacting theory.

**Calculation:** choose one conventional tachyon-free open-string setup, specify boundary conditions and Chan–Paton factors, and derive the closed-channel factorization of a four-point amplitude. Keep tadpole cancellation separate from removing physical propagation. If an orientifold is proposed, include its additional surfaces.

Then repeat the analysis in the NCOS scaling limit of T08. The original NCOS work provides a concrete one-loop comparison where the usual physical closed-string poles disappear in the stated limit [M6]. Reproducing that cancellation would identify a changed hypothesis rather than contradict ordinary open/closed consistency.

**Deliverable:** the two amplitudes, their limiting procedures, physical channels and retained couplings. State whether any limit was taken before an integral and justify exchanging them.

**Reject the route if:** removing the closed channel violates unitarity or factorization; if \(g_s\to0\) also removes all retained interactions; or if compact winding states are overlooked.

The bosonic annulus is a useful algebraic warm-up but has a tachyon. A calculation in that model alone cannot establish a stable nongravitational vacuum.

No annulus integral has been evaluated in this package. The benchmark is grounded in the source, not claimed as a new result.
