# T12 — Bound families after gravity decouples

**Priority:** P1, from the first cycle. **Status:** one-loop bound and flux-budget toy checked; geometric construction proposed.

Choose a defined class of internal theories and list the chiral zero modes before selecting a desired multiplicity. Compute the full massless spectrum and allowed mass terms. An index bounds net chirality; it does not exclude light vectorlike pairs.

For a suitable flux formulation, derive a positive charge budget nᵀQn≤2L and the linear index ℓᵀn. Audit flux quantization, anomalies, charge cancellation, stability and all directions in the lattice. Other index formulas require their own bounds; do not force them into this linear form.

Follow the same construction toward a nongravitational limit. Determine whether 2LℓᵀQ⁻¹ℓ stays bounded, whether the counted modes remain normalizable and whether extra light states appear. The algebraic toy already demonstrates how a positive but degenerating Q can erase a uniform bound.

**Deliverable:** an explicit upper bound and its domain, an enumeration of allowed family counts, and the full representations of surviving states. A stronger success derives a unique number without imposing it as an input. Three is a target to compare against, not a scan filter.

**Reject the claimed prediction if:** the flux integer is simply chosen to equal three; the compact bound does not survive the limit; family replication is identified with an untruncated oscillator tower; a scan over finitely many chosen topologies is described as a bound over all theories; or vectorlike multiplicity is silently treated as zero.

Control: with only 2N_g ordinary Dirac quarks, SU(3) asymptotic freedom gives N_g≤8. This is a useful 1974-compatible bound, not a string explanation and not sufficient for confinement. Local anomaly cancellation alone permits repeated anomaly-free generations.

Dependencies: T10 and the actual decoupling path in T01/T07/T08 or a new explicitly audited limit. Sources: H7/H8 and M15. The sharper conditional 3–8 interval additionally assumes the T11 gauge–Yukawa trajectory.
