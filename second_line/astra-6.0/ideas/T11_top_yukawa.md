# T11 — Derive one exceptional quark mass

**Priority:** P1, from the first cycle. **Status:** algebraic and RG controls executed; string construction proposed.

Specify an internal zero-mode problem and cubic interaction whose up-type Yukawa has leading rank one. Compute the full kinetic matrices and the normalized nonzero singular value. Derive the symmetry or geometry suppressing down-type Yukawas, including the bottom; a heavy member of every charge sector is not the target.

Calculate the leading rank-lifting term, its small parameter and its effect on the physical singular values. Ordinary worldvolume gauge flux alone cannot simply be assumed to lift the local holomorphic rank. Specify any extra Higgs expectation value, instanton action or supersymmetry-breaking input.

Compare the geometric boundary condition with gauge–Yukawa RG evolution through the actual particle thresholds. Use the one-loop single-Yukawa result in notes/FLAVOUR_AND_SCALES.md only as a control. Its positive fixed ratio exists for 3–8 families together with color asymptotic freedom; it neither selects three nor predicts a measured top mass.

**Deliverable:** normalized Y_u and Y_d; an input/output ledger; the predicted largest Yukawa and at least one hierarchy; variation under allowed moduli and boundary data.

**Reject the explanatory claim if:** y_t, a separate bottom suppression or a small deformation is freely fitted; normalization is omitted; RG attraction is assumed complete over a finite interval; or the same construction loses its nongravitational sector.

**Alternative:** a top-composite Higgs. Derive the binding interaction and its scale from the candidate, then solve its gap and matching equations. Record any near-critical adjustment explicitly.

Dependencies: T10 for actual representations, T12 for family constraints, T13 for the weak vacuum, and T01/T02/T09 for consistency. Sources: M12–M14, M16 and M18 in REFERENCES.md.

T14 is also a dependency whenever the hierarchy relies on supersymmetric holomorphy or protected terms. After symmetry breaking, calculate the induced corrections and establish that the physical hierarchy survives. A theorem in the supersymmetric parent is not automatically a prediction in the broken theory.
