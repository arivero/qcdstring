# T04 — Derive strings from color ordering

**Priority:** P1. **Type:** 1974 starting point; proposed microscopic construction.

Write adjoint propagators in double-line form at fixed \(\lambda=g_{\rm YM}^2N\). For an orientable connected vacuum diagram with genus \(h\) and \(b\) fundamental-matter boundaries, the color factor is \(N^{2-2h-b}\), with flavor multiplicities kept separate. This establishes the topology counting [H1].

The dynamical question is harder: when do these discrete surfaces have a continuum limit with a local or otherwise controlled worldsheet description?

**Calculation:** use cyclic single-trace words as a candidate closed-string basis. In a regulated matrix or gauge Hamiltonian, compute the leading interactions that act within one trace and the subleading terms that split or join traces. Specify the Hamiltonian rather than infer it from the topology.

Measure whether long trace states acquire a tension, an approximately local excitation spectrum and a controllable large-length limit. A matrix quantum-mechanics toy may clarify the mechanism, but it must not be called four-dimensional Yang–Mills without a derivation.

For a gauge-theory construction, impose a sharper test: reproduce the regulated Schwinger–Dyson loop identities and invariance under insertion of an exactly retraced path. For a bare lattice Wilson loop, adjacent transporters on a path and its inverse cancel identically. Test the corresponding worldsheet functional before removing the regulator; cusp and perimeter renormalization must not obscure what is being compared. The imported loop/zigzag programme supplies this diagnostic [M8].

**Deliverable:** a finite trace-basis calculation, its normalization, and a proposed scaling trajectory. Track canonically normalized glueball amplitudes \(A_k\sim N^{2-k}\) and meson amplitudes \(A_k\sim N^{1-k/2}\) when fixed-number fundamental flavors are included.

**Reject or narrow the route if:** only genus counting works, with no continuum worldsheet dynamics; or if the only successful limit is strictly \(N=\infty\) and all string interactions disappear.

The desired theory may be controlled at large finite N with an expansion in \(1/N\). There is no requirement to discard the interactions by taking the endpoint literally.

Only the elementary topological powers have been checked in this package. The trace Hamiltonian is a proposed calculation.
