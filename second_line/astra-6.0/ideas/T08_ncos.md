# T08 — An interacting open-string theory without bulk gravity

**Priority:** P1. **Type:** imported benchmark; amplitude reproduction proposed.

Use a near-critical electric field on an appropriate brane. In a common scaling convention, let

\[
\delta=1-e^2,\qquad
\alpha'=\delta\alpha'_{\rm eff},\qquad
G_o^2=g_s\sqrt{\delta}.
\]

Keep \(\alpha'_{\rm eff}\) and \(G_o\) fixed while \(\delta\to0\), with the accompanying metric scalings. Then the open-string tower retains finite tension and coupling even though

\[
\frac{G_{10}}{(\alpha'_{\rm eff})^4}\propto
G_o^4\delta^3\longrightarrow0.
\]

The power law is necessary bookkeeping, not the whole decoupling argument. The decisive benchmark is the fate of closed-channel singularities in the nonplanar amplitude [M5, M6].

**Calculation:** choose the standard noncompact electric-direction setup first. Derive the open-string spectrum, its physical metric and the one-loop nonplanar amplitude. Follow the limit with all momentum and metric factors retained.

**Deliverable:** a comparison with T02 showing where ordinary closed-string factorization is modified by the limiting procedure while the open-string interactions survive.

**Reject or restrict the route if:** the argument uses Newton-constant scaling alone; exchanges a singular limit with an integral without justification; or overlooks surviving wound closed strings after compactifying the electric direction.

This background also carries spacetime noncommutativity at the string scale. It cannot be treated as an ordinary Lorentz-invariant hadronic vacuum. Its value at this stage is a controlled test of the scope of the “strings require gravity” claim.

Only the scaling exponents have been reproduced in this package. The full one-loop check remains scheduled.
