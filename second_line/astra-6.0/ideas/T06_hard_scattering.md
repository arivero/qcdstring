# T06 — Obtain both Regge behavior and hard scattering

**Priority:** P1. **Type:** historical diagnostic plus imported mechanism.

The small-angle Regge limit and the high-energy fixed-angle limit are different. A candidate should compute both, rather than extrapolate one formula outside its domain.

The constituent-counting target is \(d\sigma/dt\sim s^{2-n}\), where \(n\) counts the leading external constituents under the usual hard-exclusive assumptions; logarithms and endpoint effects need separate attention [H6]. This is a theoretical test, not a claim that every 1974 experiment already lies in that asymptotic regime.

At fixed angle, ordinary flat-space fixed-tension string tree amplitudes have a generically exponentially soft envelope. A proposed hadronic interpretation must explain why the physical amplitude behaves differently.

**Calculation:** integrate a local string amplitude against normalizable external wavefunctions in a warped candidate background. Determine the dominant radial region as the boundary energy grows, then derive the power and angular dependence. Imported work provides this mechanism in particular confining gauge/string models [M9].

Do not insert a phenomenological energy-dependent slope independently into each channel. The local energy, tension, external wavefunctions and radial measure must follow from one model.

**Deliverable:** asymptotic formulas for fixed \(t\) and fixed \(t/s\), their domains, and a check of crossing and factorization. Use the same background already audited in T05.

**Reject the route if:** the amplitude remains exponentially soft in the intended hard regime; if the needed radial region lies outside the controlled geometry; or if fitting the exponent requires a different external-state dictionary in each process.

A successful warped calculation is not automatically a string dual of asymptotically free QCD. The relation between its ultraviolet operator dimensions and those of the desired gauge theory must also be justified.
