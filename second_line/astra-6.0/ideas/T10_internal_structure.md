# T10 — Interpret internal degrees of freedom through observables

**Priority:** P1. **Type:** proposed construction, developed alongside the nongravitational-sector audit.

Could some of the structure usually described geometrically instead encode gauge, flavor, color-ordering or renormalization data? Modern internal conformal field theories make this a concrete question, but a verbal reinterpretation does not determine a spectrum.

Choose a candidate from T07, T08 or T09. Specify its physical spacetime, internal Hilbert space, operators, boundary conditions and projections. Calculate the massless and first massive states and their charges.

**Calculation:** build a small internal current-algebra sector and calculate conformal weights and representation multiplicities. Check its embedding into the whole theory, including modular or open/closed consistency as appropriate. If chiral matter is claimed, calculate gauge anomalies; decoupling gravity does not excuse a gauge anomaly.

An anomaly-free gauge theory on a fixed background need not have a dynamical graviton. Background gravitational or mixed anomalies still require the appropriate consistency and anomaly-matching analysis if curved-background coupling is part of the claim.

**Deliverable:** an explicit state table and at least one nontrivial coupling. Mark which parameters were chosen, which follow from consistency and which would be fit to experiments available by the cutoff.

**Reject the route if:** it infers the number of generations from a dimension count, matches only an algebra without matter representations, or generates an unavoidable coupled massless spin-2 state in the physical sector it was meant to describe.

Version 0.2 promotes this test into the first cycle because the user's success criteria depend on flavour. Work on spectra and couplings proceeds alongside T01/T02; final claims still require a consistent nongravitational sector. Feed the same state table and parameters into T11 (top), T12 (families) and T13 (scales).

Technical entry points: M3–M7, M10 and the local flavour benchmarks M12–M15. No complete internal model or charge assignment is asserted in this version.
