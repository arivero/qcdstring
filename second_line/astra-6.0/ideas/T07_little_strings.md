# T07 — Keep string interactions while decoupling gravity

**Priority:** P1. **Type:** imported existence benchmark; extension proposed.

Take coincident NS5-branes in type II string theory, keep \(\alpha'\) finite and take the asymptotic coupling \(g_s\to0\). In string units, \(G_{10}/\alpha'^4\propto g_s^2\to0\). The retained five-brane theory is nontrivial; its interactions are not simply the perturbative bulk coupling [M3, M4].

This is the essential control against the idea that finite string tension alone requires dynamical gravity. Type IIA and IIB provide six-dimensional little-string laboratories with different supersymmetry. Neither is a four-dimensional hadronic model.

**Calculation:** specify the brane number, physical energy units and retained excitations. Track a finite-tension excitation, an interacting observable and bulk emission separately. In the IIB case, use the low-energy gauge dynamics as one check of a nontrivial retained sector.

Compare this with the empty-background limit \(g_s\to0\) at fixed \(\alpha'\), where ordinary perturbative string interactions vanish. The distinction is the chosen nonperturbative sector.

**Deliverable:** a table of tension, gravitational coupling, retained interaction strength and any compactification scales. Subsequently compactify two worldvolume directions and audit the four-dimensional spectrum, supersymmetry and unwanted light fields.

**Reject the phenomenological route if:** the reduction removes the finite-string regime, leaves unacceptable light sectors or offers no controlled interactions. This would not invalidate the nongravitational existence benchmark.

The script checks only the power law for the bulk Newton coupling. It does not solve little-string scattering or prove decoupling by dimensional analysis. The latter relies on the imported construction and must be reproduced more fully before extending it.
