# T09 — Find what completes the confining worldsheet

**Priority:** P0 for consistency; P2 for a complete construction.

A long string in four-dimensional field theory has two transverse translation modes. This does not make it a critical free bosonic string with four embedding coordinates. The effective theory is organized in derivatives and inverse length, and may need additional degrees of freedom at shorter scales [M1, M2].

**Calculation:** start from the leading candidate's actual worldsheet action. Audit the realization of target-space Lorentz symmetry, Weyl invariance when it is a gauge requirement, unitarity and factorization. Identify the energy at which the derivative expansion fails and what physical modes can enter there.

For a covariant critical bosonic construction with four free embedding coordinates, extra matter central charge 22 is needed. For four free RNS supercoordinates the remaining matter central charge is 9. These are consistency budgets, not direct counts of observable macroscopic dimensions.

Test candidate internal sectors, a nontrivial radial/Liouville sector, or a microscopic regulator. Each has different physical consequences. A formal central-charge balance does not by itself give a unitary, modular-invariant spectrum.

If a curved sigma model is proposed, check its beta functions. The leading metric equation has Einstein-like form [M10]. Calling the metric an auxiliary variable does not remove this consistency condition; the separate question is which target-space modes are physical observables.

**Deliverable:** a domain-of-validity statement, anomaly audit and concrete proposal for the next degrees of freedom.

**Reject the route if:** it declares \(D=4\) sufficient without the needed consistency mechanism; or if it uses the effective long-string action at arbitrarily high energy.

The central-charge arithmetic has been checked. No full worldsheet completion has been constructed here.

Joint with T14, distinguish the worldsheet supercurrent from physical spacetime supercharges. If extra light fermions are claimed on a confining flux tube, compute their boundary conditions and contribution to its finite-size spectrum. The new note gives free NS/R Casimir controls. The physical transverse theory used in that test is not the unprojected covariant RNS theory with its ghosts discarded.
