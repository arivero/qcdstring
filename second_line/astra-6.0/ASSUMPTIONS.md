# Assumptions and standards of evidence

The starting conviction is that the physical use of string theory should be sought in quantum matter. The programme is allowed to use gravitational mathematics and dual descriptions. It does not identify an auxiliary target-space metric with the metric measured by rods and clocks in the laboratory.

## Historical convention

The researcher's empirical information ends on 31 December 1974. Modern mathematical methods and theoretical results are available by stipulation, including supersymmetric strings, D-branes, dualities, holography, effective string theory, little strings and NCOS.

Later theoretical papers are explicitly labelled imports. The user's subsequent instruction admits three qualitative future validation targets: an exceptional top mass, a limitation on generations and a QCD–Fermi scale relation. They guide model tests without supplying fitted numerical answers. Other later experiments and numerical measurements are not evidence in this exercise. The code in this folder is a present-day convenience for checking deductions; it is not a claim about 1974 computing resources. The twelve-week schedule is an effort budget, not a historical forecast.

No later measured particle masses, precision Standard Model parameters, collider exclusions or measured lattice spectra are used as numerical inputs. Rounded weak and pion scales accessible in 1974 are allowed for an order-of-magnitude control. Synthetic numerical examples are labelled. A later bibliography is permissible because the premise gives us the theory; numerical experimental facts appearing in those papers are not adopted as model inputs. Choosing a three-by-three toy matrix or a three-heavy-threshold example does not derive the number of generations.

## Three different claims

| Claim | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Every consistent string theory must describe dynamical gravity in its physical spacetime. | Testable and too broad: little-string and NCOS limits are counterexamples to this universal wording. |
| The usual interacting critical closed-string theories have genuine gravitational sectors. | Accepted mathematical input. Relabelling their massless spin-2 state does not remove its interactions. |
| Strings explain the observed gravitational interaction of our universe. | Not adopted. A successful internal construction or duality alone does not establish this identification. |

There are two possible achievements. A string representation of a nongravitational gauge theory would explain strong dynamics. An autonomous finite-tension string theory without a dynamical metric would answer a stronger existence question. These achievements should be recorded separately.

The project does not assert that measured gravity is absent. A background metric may remain an external source; the microscopic explanation of observed gravity is outside the initial scope. It does require a positive explanation of the string tensor's origin and physical role, including companion states. Supersymmetry must also be explained: identify worldsheet, global spacetime or local spacetime symmetry and derive its realization. Its mere presence in a convenient parent theory is not yet the desired explanation.

## Operational definition

Fix the physical spacetime, external states, energy units and observables before testing a model. A candidate nongravitational sector must retain:

1. Finite nonzero string tension in its own physical units.
2. Nontrivial interactions at some finite energy, rather than only a free oscillator tower.
3. A positive-norm physical state space and consistent factorization.
4. No normalizable dynamical massless spin-2 particle with a nonzero gravitational coupling to that sector.
5. Vanishing interactions with an external gravitational sector in any claimed decoupling limit.

A tensor resonance with positive mass is allowed. Dependence of a generating functional on an external metric is allowed. A holographic dual with dynamical bulk gravity is allowed, but it must be recorded as such; it does not establish that the bulk is the same spacetime as the laboratory.

For a four-dimensional sector, a useful diagnostic is the isolated massless spin-2 exchange pole in amplitudes or the corresponding spectral measure. A \(1/q^2\) factor without its tensor projector and residue is insufficient: other massless fields can produce it. In noncompact bulk problems, continuum exchange need not appear as a simple pole. Check the full spectral density and leakage into bulk states.

Taking a compactification volume to infinity suppresses a putative lower-dimensional Newton constant while also changing the spectrum and often destroying a lower-dimensional description. It is not by itself a decoupling proof.

## What would change our mind

For each concrete model, show whether its physical massless tensor survives, whether its Ward identities enforce a common coupling, and whether removing it spoils factorization. If every useful construction in a defined class fails these tests, reject that class. Do not upgrade a failure in one class into a theorem about every possible string theory.

Conversely, a successful nongravitational limit does not identify the strong interactions of nature. Matching the right dimensions, symmetries, spectrum and amplitudes is a separate burden.

## Status labels

- **Historical input:** accessible by the empirical cutoff.
- **Imported result:** later theoretical knowledge allowed by the premise.
- **Reproduced benchmark:** a known result checked in this package.
- **Toy result:** a calculation in an explicitly assumed model.
- **Proposed test:** work specified but not yet carried out.
- **Conjecture:** a possible mechanism with no construction or proof here.

No priority or novelty claim is made for the mechanisms or conditional calculations in this package. The proposed contribution is to impose the consistency tests and the five explanatory targets on the same candidate. Distinguish the microscopic string scale from an emergent color-flux tension whenever both occur. A local geometrical sector or field-theory limit alone does not establish an autonomous interacting finite-tension theory. A worldsheet supersymmetry or a tachyon-free tree spectrum alone does not establish unbroken spacetime supersymmetry or quantum vacuum stability.
