# qcdstring

**Date of founding: 6 November 1974.**

Working premise of this repository: the relativistic string of the dual
resonance model is the chromoelectric flux tube of the strong
interactions. Its tension is set by the Regge slope, alpha' ~ 0.9 GeV^-2,
and that is the only scale the theory has ever been anchored to by data.
The proposal of Scherk, Schwarz and Yoneya (this year) to reinterpret the
massless spin-2 closed-string state as the graviton, by rescaling alpha'
by thirty-eight orders of magnitude, is treated here as a wrong turn.
The arguments for and against are laid out in `THESIS.md`.

The author of this repository has an unusual handicap: a complete memory
of how the subject develops over the next fifty years. That memory is a
resource, and it is used openly. Results that are "remembered" rather
than derived are labelled as such everywhere, and are treated as
conjectures until re-derived with the tools and data of 1974. A memory
of a result is a strong hint about *what* is true and a weak hint about
*why*; the research plan is built around closing that gap.

The retrospective comparisons use the actual publication dates. The original author was GPT-6.2 Astra and the independent textual referee was GPT-6.1 Sol. Subsequent review was also by AI. The latest biennial article is **Where does the color go when a string is lifted?** (QCDS-98/1). It follows a fundamental source through the 1995 duality web and the 1997 fivebrane construction. Free bulk winding and the bound strip have the same relative center charge. Their tensions and supersymmetry properties differ. The free-end calculation predicts an internal rotational level in the fixed membrane probe and identifies the scale obstruction to carrying it into four-dimensional Yang–Mills. The **1999 odd-year supplement** asks what the string at the tip of the 1998 compactified D4 cigar carries. It derives the complete quadratic spectrum, fermions included: a charged doublet at $M_{\rm KK}$, eight fermions at $M_{\rm KK}/2$ from the four-form flux, and four sphere fields that form an $O(5)$ sigma model with an exponentially small gap. In the controlled regime the tube carries six light transverse bosons, and its Casimir term is three times the value for two translations. Its spin-structure difference realizes the massive case of P21 at quadratic order. Symmetry forbids the charged cigar quantum to decay directly into two fermions. The string clauses of P32 are reached; its bulk clause is narrowed to one computed tower. The D4 route to a pure four-dimensional spectrum is narrowed. The **2025 odd-year supplement** follows the QCD string through breaking. Its third edition matches the first nonconstant internal mode to a four-dimensional colour-field insertion. It derives an isospin zero in two-flavour QCD and a nonzero strange-quark contribution to the short-distance potential. A separate scalar probe compares the measured ground and excited levels with the light and strange screening thresholds at 1.08–1.33 fm. The displacement experiment shows what an internal preference would look like when the endpoints move. Its row energies are imposed. The QCD action-density measurements follow the tube in ordinary space. ## Conventions

* Writing: avoid personal possessive attribution of theories and ideas; use neutral wording or collective first person. See [the project writing note](AGENTS.md).

* Units: GeV, with hbar = c = 1. alpha' is the Regge slope; sigma = 1/(2 pi alpha') is the string tension.
* Remembered numbers are predictions, never inputs or evidence (see `second_line/README.md` for the mapping onto the second line's six status labels).
* Every claim is tagged with one of: `[1974]` derivable or checkable now; `[MEMORY]` remembered from the future, to be rederived; `[TEST]` a concrete check that can be run.
* Idea files are numbered and never renumbered. Status lines are one of `open`, `in progress`, `supported`, `refuted`, `superseded by NNN`.
* Papers are cited by the key in the manifests (e.g. `Wilson1974`).
