# P30 · A displaced fundamental colour string through screening

This record precedes the numerical experiment for the revised 2025 supplement. The earlier edition is tagged y2025. We fix a two-row spatial strip with SU(3) link rotors, fundamental screening matter and two static sources in the singlet sector. A fundamental occupied horizontal link costs 1 on row A or 2 on B. Each occupied rung costs 1.25. A light endpoint costs 3.25. Define the electric insertion on each central horizontal link as its quadratic Casimir divided by 4/3, averaging the two central bonds for even L. Move both static sources from A to B and hold the action fixed. In the uniform control both horizontal costs are 1.

The colour-electric calculation retains simple longitudinally monotone strings and all their cuts, including multiple light pairs. A local gauge-invariant matter hop connects the trivial and fundamental link representations. Its traced hopping coefficient is t, giving a one-link projected amplitude t/3 in the static-pair sector. The projected model has no fitted string-to-meson matrix element. Compute both lowest eigenstates. Subtract the two single-source sectors and add the vacuum for the central insertion. Keep the raw values available.

**Predictions.** At zero hopping the displaced tube chooses between costs 2L and L+2.5. The screened cost is 6.5. Thus for displaced sources the midpoint lies on A at L=3, while the ground state at L=5 is screened and the first excited state carries flux on A. We predict the following persist at t=0.45: the subtracted central A signal exceeds 0.70 for the displaced ground state at 3, is smaller than 0.25 in magnitude for the displaced ground state at 5, and exceeds 0.65 in its first excitation. At 3 the on-A ground state also exceeds 0.70 on A. In the homogeneous control the displacement moves the signal with the sources. Report results even if they violate these bounds.

At L=4 with sources on B, tune twice the endpoint mass near 6.5 to the minimum gap of the two lowest levels. The unique minimal path has four A links and two rungs. A local matter hop must act on all six to mix this string with the completely screened state. For growing subsets of occupied edges on that path the intermediate electric-plus-matter energy exceeds the crossing energy. The leading gap is twice the sum over orders of the six hops, each with factor (t/3)^6 and five inverse excitation energies. Compare that coefficient with diagonalization at t=0.45, 0.30 and 0.225. Determine the midpoint matrix elements on both sides and at the tuned crossing. Test the local insertion independently by differentiating the corresponding link coefficient away from the small gap. Compare a reflected strip and matched screened references. These are tests of a strongly coupled, finite SU(3) model with scalar rotor matter. The couplings are specified model inputs.
