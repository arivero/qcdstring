We retain the two held-out residuals, with all seven inputs and their common energy origin specified. An independent decimal-arithmetic calculation now obtains both missing roots by quadratic deflation. [Verification](results/verification.json)
2. We supplied the missing primary-source register. It gives the versioned source URL, exact archive members, acquisition and extraction commands, three SHA-256 digests, axis units, tick positions, marker selection and identification of fitted bands. The [registration commit](https://github.com/arivero/qcdstring/commit/5a558ca8dbe9f715ec8a15a267e7261817db9dc0) preceded the extraction and tests on this branch. The measurements were already publicly available. The check reserves measurements within our procedure. [Source register](SOURCES.md)
3. The abstract and conclusion now identify the QCD string by the rising energy and extended field distribution over a range of separations. They state that the ground state becomes screened. Section 8 and Appendix B distinguish a gauge-action measurement from normalized physical energy and show how a transition matrix element affects the local response.
4. The revised conclusion dates the finite-temperature free-energy evidence to the 1998 preprint and the two-flavour zero-temperature spectrum to 2005. We cite Prkacin et al. separately for spatial action densities. Section 3.4 preserves the explicit limitation on reconnection in the connected classical holographic solution. [Historical evidence](SOURCES.md)
5. Section 7 and Appendix A specify the graphical precision, the shared static-light subtraction and the absence of a joint covariance in the digitized figure. The conditioned level does not count as a successful prediction. Its residual is identically zero. The analytic sensitivity to its input was also checked against numerical derivatives. [Author checks](AUTHOR_CHECKS.md)
