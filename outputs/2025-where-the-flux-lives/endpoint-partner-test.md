# An antiquark and a scalar diquark at the same static colour source

This contemporary test asks which part of the massive BPS partner relation could be measured in the Wilson theory. The 1995 calculation has a bulk supercharge preserved by the limiting vortex. The 1986 endpoint proposal assigns a fermionic transition to an antiquark and a diquark with equal colour. The 1998 fivebrane string can break every charge preserved by its bulk. Each string has its own charge algebra and source normalization. ## Endpoint fields and a transition with baryon number one

Use a static fundamental source $H^a$ with the same temporal link action in both channels. Factor out its spin. With $a,b,c$ colour and $i,j,k$ three active light flavours, take
$$
 A_k=H^a\bar q_{ak},\qquad
 D_k=\tfrac12 H^a\epsilon_{abc}\epsilon_{kij}
                   q_{bi}^{\mathsf T}C\gamma_5 q_{cj}.
$$
Use local fields or transport every separated constituent to the same endpoint. Both are colour singlets. The light clouds are a spin-$1/2$ antiquark and a spin-zero positive-parity diquark. The antisymmetric spin contraction and colour epsilon give an antisymmetric flavour pair. Thus both transform as $\bar{\bf3}$ of vector flavour for three equal masses. For six active flavours the antisymmetric pair has dimension 15 and a flavour restriction or threshold matching is required. With a positive-parity static quark and its spin restored, the lowest light $j^P=1/2^-$ meson gives the $0^-,1^-$ doublet, while the $0^+$ diquark gives the $1/2^+$ static baryon. For spinless source grading the operators are respectively odd and even. Using a common fermionic heavy source flips both gradings.

Count light baryon number in units of one for three quarks. The two labels have $-1/3,+2/3$, respectively. A transition taking the first into the second carries $+1$, is odd and has a spinor component. At an unbroken vector-flavour point the flavour singlet can act diagonally in $k$. One candidate insertion with the required quantum numbers is $\epsilon_{abc}\epsilon_{ijk}(q_{Li}^{a\,\mathsf T} i\sigma_2 q_{Lj}^b)q_{Rk}^c$ together with the parity partner for the specified projection. The opposite Weyl chirality in the third slot avoids the vanishing total antisymmetrization of three two-component spin indices. The operator is a candidate baryon interpolator. Constructing a conserved charge with a normalized surface flux is an additional requirement. The release supplies the colour epsilon, the grading and separated-correlator tests for odd local fields. Extended static lines, their contact terms and this transition require their own matching. ## A ratio that tests the action of the charge

Choose creator conventions for $A,D$ as above, and a putative integrated transition $\mathcal Q$ of baryon number $+1$. In the middle of a common static line the neutral numerator is $\langle D_k^\dagger(T)\mathcal Q(T/2+s) A_k(0)\rangle$. Divide by $\sqrt{C_D(T)C_A(T)}$ for the corresponding diagonal correlators in the same static action. For isolated lowest states with nonzero overlaps and a fixed offset $s$, the ratio tends, up to the known endpoint overlap phases, to
$$
 e^{(E_d-E_a)s}\langle d|\mathcal Q|a\rangle .
$$
This follows by inserting the transfer resolution on either side of the transition. Equal static lengths cancel the common additive self-energy. The ratio removes the magnitudes of the source and sink overlaps. The normalization of the transition and its line contact still require a separate prescription. The complete product of fields and lines must be inserted *before* applying each normalized blocking map, including in the conjugate ordering. [Joint-insertion transfer, §97.4](https://github.com/yang-mills-group/OpenAI-informal-20261115/blob/6ca21d98b4022263fd7f2a3de5b5913b8495ee02/continuum-yang-mills-theory.pdf)

For a conserved defect charge, a nonzero matrix element requires $E_d=E_a$ and is independent of $s$. A possible rest-frame supersymmetry normalization is $\{\mathcal Q,\mathcal Q^\dagger\}=2(H_{\rm stat}-E_{\rm ref})$. If $\mathcal Q^\dagger|a\rangle=0$ and the partner saturates the channel, the squared matrix element is $2(E_a-E_{\rm ref})$. Otherwise the relation is a sum rule over intermediate states. Fix the charge and common energy origin once, or compare differences of the sum rule between two levels so the origin cancels. An unrestricted baryon interpolator can have a nonzero three-point function without satisfying the offset and residue tests.

A published indication of the size of the energy test is the static $us$ or $ds$ scalar-diquark state relative to a light static meson. The two valence strange discretizations in Table 6 give $602(33)$ and $629(41)$ MeV with the $0.079$ fm scale setting. Here the sea contains two light flavours, the strange quark is valence, and the light results are extrapolated. These nonzero differences disfavor exact energy pairing in that calculation. They leave the equal-three-flavour Ward test and the supersymmetric vortex result distinct. [Wagner and Wiese, Tables 1 and 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.4921#page=18)

## A second response under a mass deformation

Use the renormalized deformation $M(\lambda)=M(0)+\lambda\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-2)$ at fixed gauge reference, geometry and static action. The derivative of $E_d-E_a$ is the difference of the two vacuum-subtracted, matched scalar insertions per unit line time. Both line-identity counterterms cancel. At a flavour-symmetric member the responses within each anti-triplet have the form $g_A t_k$ and $g_D t_k$, with $(t_k)=(1,1,-2)$ and any anti-representation sign absorbed into $g$. Flavour symmetry leaves the two reduced coefficients independent.

If a charge commutes with the Hamiltonian along a neighbourhood of this mass deformation and connects the two isolated states, differentiating $(E_d-E_a)\langle d|\mathcal Q|a\rangle=0$ at the paired point gives $g_D=g_A$. If the deformation explicitly breaks that charge, the matrix element of $[\dot H,\mathcal Q]$ instead equals $(\dot E_d-\dot E_a)\langle d|\mathcal Q|a\rangle$ for exact partners with no additional images in the two channels. Degenerate states require the projected matrices in their whole eigenspaces. This compares the scalar response and the three-point residue at the same source normalization. It requires a regulator-uniform line insertion and the large-time derivative control of the static mass note. Their values on three-degenerate-flavour ensembles remain open.
