# QCDS-25/S1 · Registered diagnostics

December 2025 in the series. This record fixes the predictions before running our calculations. Witten (1997), Bulava et al. (2024) and Giliberti et al. (2024) are published inputs. The published fit already motivated the choice of a three-channel Hamiltonian. Its parameters and spectra generated from them cannot serve as independent data.

**P28.** Place two opposite static Abelian sources with the same normalized internal density \(1+a\cos\theta\) on a flat circle of radius \(B\), in three noncompact spatial dimensions. Let \(y=L/B\). For the same force normalization at \(a=0\), the profiles \(+a\) and \(-a\) give identical forces, with \(F/F_0=1+(a^2/2)(1+y)e^{-y}\). Their normalized first moments of the electric energy density at the ordinary midpoint have opposite signs and magnitude \(|A|/(1+A^2/2)\), \(A=a(1+y/2)e^{-y/2}\). The source Fourier coefficients enter the exchange twice and the midpoint field once. Test \(a=\pm0.6,\ y=2\) against integration of the compact Green function over both sources and the square of the local field. Target absolute error below \(10^{-7}\) on the two ratios. This is a weak-field test.

**P29.** If a three-level real Hermitian static Hamiltonian varies with separation only in one diagonal string entry, in an energy-independent basis, the normalized difference of the monic spectral cubics at any two distinct traces is the same fixed monic quadratic. Its roots are the two eigenvalues of the compression to the orthogonal subspace. Expansion of the determinant fixes the squared couplings at these roots. One level at another separation then fixes the other two unless the input equals a decoupled threshold. Require positive squared couplings, real distinct roots, interlacing and consistent held-out levels. For the algebraic control use Table 4 central fit parameters of Bulava et al. in their flow units, separations \(r/\sqrt{t_0}=7,8,9\), train on 7 and 9, condition on the middle eigenvalue at 8. Require absolute error below \(10^{-8}\) in the other levels. This tests the inverse, not QCD. For a data check seek the three measured levels closest to the light crossing on ensemble N200 at \(r/a=17,19,21\), calibrating on the outside two and reserving 19. Use a common energy origin. Report missing levels, digitization and covariance limits or failure as such. Do not substitute fitted curves for measurements. Any alternative data selection is exploratory.
