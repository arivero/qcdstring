## Displaced-source revision

- The [P30 registration](https://github.com/arivero/qcdstring/commit/827633aca323713e2e58e09c9769b6c03b602f2f) preceded construction and diagonalization of the strip. Its finite-hopping bounds, uniform control and gap comparisons are reported with the original parameters.
- We rebuilt both published CSVs from the versioned arXiv archives in a separate temporary directory. Rerunning all analysis programs there reproduced the nine archived CSV, numerical-result and plot files byte for byte under a single BLAS thread.
- Direct matrix elements and a finite difference of the four-sector energy agree to within 9 × 10⁻¹¹ for both levels at separations three and five. Reflecting both features and sources reproduces the comparison within 2 × 10⁻¹⁵. [Finite strip results](results/strip.json)
- A rational sum over 720 orders independently reproduces the subset-recursion coefficient. The surrounding gaps and the 15-point coarse mass scan plus local points bound the observed stationary minimum from both sides. [Verification](results/strip-verification.json)
- The full-Hamiltonian second-order formula is derived in Section 9.4 and evaluated arithmetically. A separate SU(3) Weyl-matrix sum checks the Haar moments used there. In an isolated link with distant spectator mesons, diagonalization with complete product contractions removes the spurious ordered-basis offsets exactly. The comparable error of the full connected strip is unknown. [Local checks](results/strip-local.json)
- We checked the 26-page revised PDF, the bounds of each page, both scientific figures and links against y2025-v2. The original y2025 tag records the first edition.

## First harmonic revision

- The public mode and mass-scan registration precedes the first signed eigenfunction calculation and extraction of D200 and N203 for this paper. N200 had already appeared in the earlier edition. The strange-loop consequence was registered after the mass scan and before its independent numerical test. [First registration](https://github.com/arivero/qcdstring/commit/081ad4acdd71f42351ca35662afe4bf62c12d3b3) · [Loop registration](https://github.com/arivero/qcdstring/commit/a609c0dc8b69ac33218f99f556afc5481442c8df)
- Separate background integrators and half-meridian shooting and finite elements give the odd and even signed overlaps. The neck positive-partner differences at step 0.01 are below $2.1\times10^{-7}$. The cap-32 and round-limit checks are in the stored output. The analytic parity cancellation is imposed in the half-meridian integration. For an unconstrained check we used the full-meridian cubic-spline basis of the pinned companion revision. Its two lowest nonconstant modes give right overlaps of about $-3.5\times10^{-10},+0.09423715$ and positive-partner overlaps $+1.28211457,-0.97685389$. [Stored half-meridian runs](results/harmonics.json) · [Pinned full-meridian implementation](https://github.com/arivero/conneskk/blob/6ac5bfa16f927c44c500509a6a3849e83704d84e/paper/weak_current_spectrum.py)
- For the full-meridian check call `grid, _, F, dF = spline_grid_basis(.001, .125, 25.)`. Diagonalize the symmetric generalized problem with matrices `dF.T @ (w[:,None]*dF)` and `F.T @ ((w*f2)[:,None]*F)`, using the grid weights `w` and metric `f2`. Normalize profiles to unit area mean square, orient the odd one north-positive and the even one equator-positive, and average each against the separately normalized right and left densities of (33). No parity constraint enters these matrices.
- We checked both kinetic insertion sites by a symmetric finite difference of the static-to-light propagator squared. The Feynman-parameter and timelike-spectral computations agree to relative $5.6\times10^{-13}$ across all seven points. After the referee request we retained both heavy denominators in the dispersion transform and verified the approach to the soft coefficient at nine positive mass/scale pairs.
