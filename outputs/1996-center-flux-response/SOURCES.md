# Sources and historical cutoff

The paper is set in December 1996. Its running equations use the following primary material.

| Item | Role |
|---|---|
| [Seiberg and Witten (1994)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9407087) and [Argyres and Faraggi (1994 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9411057) | The magnetic effective theory, the electric charge pairing and the pure higher-rank curve. |
| [Douglas and Shenker (1995), §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9503163) | Eqs. (2.6)–(2.10) specify cycles and periods. Eqs. (2.17)–(2.22) and §3 give the quadratic condensates, elementary tensions and screening. Our monic polynomial is twice their polynomial after restoring the scale. The quartic source and screened scan are calculated in this article. |
| [Taubes (1980)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01197552) | Critical Abelian vortices at prescribed positions attain the square-completion bound in a factor. |
| [QCDS-95/S1](../1995-electric-string-corollary/paper.md) and [the 1994 working note](../1994-monopole-string/monopole-vacuum.md) | Earlier electric source, normalization and finite-deformation limitations for two colors. |
| [Townsend (1995)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9501068), [Witten (1995)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9503124) and [Strominger (1995 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9512059) | The IIA lift, circle wrapping and the possibility of a membrane boundary on a fivebrane. The relative homology condition in §9 is calculated under its stated hypotheses. |
| [Hanany and Witten (November 1996 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9611230) | The three-dimensional IIB construction was available at the historical cutoff. |

The retrospective comparison cites [Edelstein et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0001184) for the quadratic and cubic perturbations, [Auzzi and Konishi](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0205172) for finite-mass corrections, and the later [fivebrane](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9706109) and [MQCD-string](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9707244) papers. These sources play no role in the numerical input or the 1996 derivation. Literature priority for the particular quartic-source experiment is unassessed.
