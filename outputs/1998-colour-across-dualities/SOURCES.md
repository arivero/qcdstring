# Historical source record

The December 1998 article responds to physics that had already appeared. The following distinctions matter for its claims.

- The 1995 duality arguments supply the IIA lift, the IIB charge norm and the heterotic–type I map. Polchinski and Witten construct the 1–1 and 1–9 worldsheet modes. Hořava and Witten describe a different heterotic string as a membrane stretched across an interval. [IIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9503124) · [IIB](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9508143) · [Type I](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9510169) · [Interval](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9510209)
- In Witten (1997), §§2.2–3.2 give the embedding, flat metric, rotation, homotopy from bulk winding to the bound strip, both relative homology groups, four Goldstinos and the angular winding problem. Section 5 addresses the conflict of scales. These are established sources, including the parametric stiffness estimate. [Branes and the dynamics of QCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9706109)
- Hanany, Strassler and Zaffaroni derive the equal-circle sine chord and explicitly identify the nearby orthogonal line in §4 footnote 3. Our P27 calculation varies both endpoint arguments and matches the projected kinetic norm on their minimizer. The formulae refer to the fixed classical action. [Confinement and strings in MQCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9707244)
- The quadratic sine relation and the registered four-color quartic perturbation of the 1996 article refer to a weak monopole vacuum. A radius change in P27 has not been identified with that quartic source. [Previous article](../1996-center-flux-response/paper.md)
- The 1998 compactified D4 construction supplies a nonsupersymmetric comparison. The baryon-vertex example in the 1998 AdS paper uses a different brane. Section 8 explicitly integrates the D4 worldvolume coupling in the D4 background. The conformal D3 Wilson loop is a separate example of a color source with zero asymptotic tension. [D4](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9803131) · [Baryon vertex](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9805112) · [Wilson loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9803002)
