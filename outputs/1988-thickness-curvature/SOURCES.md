# Sources for QCDS-88/1

Machine retrieval date: 17 November 2026. Scientific cutoff: 30 November 1988.
The source manifest is `sources.tsv`. No post-1988 scientific result is an
input. All new calculations are derived in the paper.

## Reading coverage

- QCDS-84/1: the curvature classification, scattering conventions, stated
  limits and response to the referee. QCDS-86/1: Section 3.3, the massive
  determinant construction, conclusion, source record and author checks.
  Earlier simulations were not rerun.
- Kleinert1986: the four-page published PDF was retrieved from the author
  website and verified with `pdfinfo`. Printed p. 335 (PDF p. 1) was read
  visually, including equations (1)-(2), after `pdftotext -layout` returned no
  useful text. The original and selected-reading digest are in the ignored
  `../../papers/pdf/Kleinert1986.pdf` and `../../papers/md/Kleinert1986.md`.
  Pages 336-338, including the running-coupling calculation, were not reviewed.
- Polyakov1986: the publisher abstract supplied by INSPIRE and the Princeton
  publication record were read; title, DOI, year and page range were checked.
  INSPIRE provides no original download for this record. A targeted public
  search did not locate an original; full-text coverage is not claimed.
- AlonsoEspriu1987: the publisher abstract supplied by INSPIRE and the
  publisher landing page were read. The metadata establish the authors as
  F. Alonso and D. Espriu. The classical and large-dimension calculations
  mentioned in the abstract were not read in full or used as equations here.

The selected historical statements concern the scope of those papers. The
present paper does not claim to reproduce their running-coupling results or
to rule out every Euclidean statistical-surface construction. Its pole test
concerns a specified quadratic propagator interpreted as a physical field.
