# Sources for QCDS-91/S1

The physical inputs to the calculation were available through December 1990. The paper is dated November 1991 and credits a contemporary calculation as a comparison. The quoted profile and scattering numbers are generated from the microscopic action. The first reading was on 18 November 2026. The later source checks are dated separately.

- Nielsen and Olesen (1973), *Vortex-line models for dual strings*. The public scan of the original was read online in §2, especially printed pp. 47–49, equations (2.6)–(2.16), for the boundary conditions, circulation and radial equations. Our sign of the flux follows our sign in the covariant derivative. The pole calculation is separate. [Public scan](https://infomall.org/sites/dsc/jpac/QCDRef/1970s/Vortex-line%20models%20for%20dual%20strings%20-%20Nielsen%2C%20Olesen%20-%201973.pdf) · [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(73)90350-7)
- Davis (1987), *Twisted vortices, string solitons, and world sheets instantons*. The publisher abstract identifies the massive two-dimensional photon and flux twist. This establishes a period precedent for the physical mechanism. We do not attribute our normalized two-translation overlap or its numerical value to that abstract. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(87)90611-0)
- Arodź (1991), *Bound states of the vector field with a vortex in the Abelian Higgs model*. Read the university scan, printed pp. 511–520 and the concluding bibliography on p. 521. Sections 2–3 give the bound-mode equation and Proca energy. Section 4 discusses the scalar potential and the note added in proof credits Davis. Local text extraction used pdftotext with layout preservation. The OCR of Greek indices and subscripts is imperfect. The printed page 516 was visually checked for the bound-state argument, the expansion (19) and the constraint (21). On 24 November we also checked the typeset angular-momentum formula on p. 518, its plane measure and its normalization against the QCDS-91/S1 flux integral. All displayed equations in QCDS-91/S1 are derived with its own conventions. The local reading copy and its companion use the ignored papers shelf. [University scan](https://ruj.uj.edu.pl/server/api/core/bitstreams/9c989e24-b7c6-4852-8608-2ed28f1defc6/content)
- De Vega and Schaposnik (1976) give the equal-mass vortex. Their publisher abstract and bibliographic record were consulted. Simon (1976) treats weak binding in the plane. We give the square completion and log-trial proof in full. [Equal-mass vortex](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.14.1100) · [Planar bound state](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(76)90038-5)
- Mazur and Nair (1987) give historical context for the normal-bundle density. The coupling and external-state conventions used here come from the 1990 note of the programme. [1987 article](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(87)90030-7) · [1990 amplitude note](../1990-pseudoscalar-curvature/amplitudes.md)
- The 1995 preprint on nonlinear radiation and the 2026 thesis abstract on Abelian flux tubes were consulted for the retrospective note. The preprint introduction and bibliography also helped locate the two period accounts of the longitudinal mode. The original calculation used its own boundary conditions, matrix elements and numerical inputs. Later literature supplies the retrospective comparisons below. [Arodź and Hadasz](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9506021) · [Gaikwad](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7ms0x3br)

## Additional source checks, 24 November 2026

- **The factor of two.** Compared the March and journal displays directly. March p. 4 and journal p. 096010-5 give the same field ansatz (16) and opposite signs for the off-diagonal Hessian (20).
