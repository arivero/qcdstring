# Sources for the 1995 electric-string corollary

The historical argument uses the 1994 Seiberg–Witten periods and monopole superpotential and the 1995 discussion of Abelian confinement by Douglas and Shenker. Local vortex results cited as QCDS-91/S1 and QCDS-92/1 belong to this programme. Subsequent publications are identified in the retrospective.

| Result | Source and scope |
|---|---|
| Fundamental Wilson line ends one primitive dual vortex | §3.2 of Seiberg–Witten fixes the dual Bianchi multiplier and the electric weight normalization. §5.6 gives the monopole vacuum; §6.2 gives the period integral. The expansion, charge calculation and scale conversion are written out in §§2–4 of the present paper. [1994 primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9407087) |
| Bulk and defect scales | Douglas–Shenker discuss the spectrum of Abelian string tensions at weak breaking and its limitations. We specialize to one light magnetic factor. [1995 primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9503163) |
| Trace doublet and critical odd residue | The preceding articles derive the on-shell normalization and the local scalar splitting. The present work uses their wave normalizations, not a fit to hadronic data. [1991 pole](../1991-vortex-pseudoscalar/paper.md) · [1992 doublet](../1992-scalar-doublet/paper.md) |
| Additional odd state, its displacement and mixing | The imaginary charged and neutral fields follow from (3). Eq. (16) is tested by a new resolvent solve and by direct finite-deformation diagonalization. The regular gauge-kinetic coefficient extracted from the periods is also compared with Eq. (6) of the later Vainshtein–Yung matching. The number assigned to an individual mixed pole holds only in the specified one-operator calculation. [Working derivation](../1994-monopole-string/pseudoscalar.md) · [Retrospective comparison](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0012250v2) |
| Prior odd-mode mechanisms | Arodź (1991) and Arodź–Hadasz (1995 preprint) address vector localization and subsequent nonlinear radiation. Agia (2015) studies the neutral field–longitudinal vector mixing. The 2026 Abelian-Higgs preprint derives both normalized curvature couplings at criticality. These works and the Yang–Mills results concern different microscopic field content. [Arodź–Hadasz](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9506021) · [Agia, §4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.03404) · [Critical couplings](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26013v1) |

The corpus holds local reading copies of the 1994 and 1995 gauge-theory papers. This publication distributes the links, calculations and original figure.
