# Review response status

There was no independent referee for QCDS-86/1; accordingly there is no response
to a referee. The user expressly requested author checks and publication without
that review. See [REFEREE.md](REFEREE.md).

During author preparation the paper incorporated these qualifications:

- The fixed-mass determinant and the self-consistent Gross-Neveu saddle are
  different models. The latter includes the condensation-energy difference.
- The homogeneous large-flavour transition is not a finite-flavour or QCD
  transition; Dashen, Ma and Rajaraman's 1975 limitation is stated.
- A periodic scalar-Majorana cancellation prevents interpreting a single
  vacuum Casimir coefficient as an unrestricted mode census.
- Nonchiral mass generation is not applied to an isolated chiral heterotic
  sector or advertised as a solution of the critical Weyl anomaly.
- Negative mass dimension above four dimensions is not a proof of triviality.
  Compactification leaves the parent's ultraviolet problem and unwanted zero
  modes to be addressed. Small compact target coordinates need not be gapped
  on the sheet.
- The earlier three-dimensional data are not promoted to evidence about
  four-dimensional QCD. The 1984 higher-order tasks remain explicitly open.
- The reported 2026 Lean proof is not used. Source access and selected reading
  are distinguished from full-text review.

These are author decisions and checks, not requested referee corrections.

On resumption, at the user request to review and enhance the draft, Section
8.4 was added. It constructs a periodic pinning model, distinguishes the
classical Hessian mass from the quantum mass, and derives the free-field
relevance test. This gives a restricted failure condition for combining weak
pinning with a parametrically small geometric radius. The model is not
presented as spontaneous compactification. Additional author checks cover
the Hessian and Gaussian shell average. The prose was revised to follow the
project-wide nonpossessive attribution rule in `../../AGENTS.md`.
